How it works

Practice Jazz Chords is a jazz chord trainer. Every bar it shows a chord symbol, such as Cmaj7, G7 or Dm7, in a new key. Play it in time with the click and glance at the next chord to get ready. Rate the keys you know, and the ones you still need to practice come up more often.

Practice single chords (maj7, m7 and 7), the ii–V–I in major and minor, Imaj7–vi–ii–V, or write your own progression in Roman numerals. Choose how the keys follow each other: at random, in weighted rounds, or around the circle of fourths. Everything runs in the browser, free and without an account.
